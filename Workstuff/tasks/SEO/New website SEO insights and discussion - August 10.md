### Technical SEO Best Practices

- [**Auditing Tools:**](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=587.0 "PLAY @9:47")
    - [**Screaming Frog:** For technical issues (broken links, redirects, schemas).](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=587.0 "PLAY @9:47")
    - [**Ahrefs:** For general SEO analysis.](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=587.0 "PLAY @9:47")
    - [**Google PageSpeed Insights:** For mobile and desktop performance.](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=1209.0 "PLAY @20:09")
- [**Core Principles:**](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=901.0 "PLAY @15:01")
    - **Semantic HTML:** Use proper HTML structure (e.g., `<h1>`, `<h2>`) over JavaScript.
        - [**Rationale:** AI engines (Gemini, ChatGPT) struggle to render JavaScript, which can prevent indexing.](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=960.0 "PLAY @16:00")
    - [**Canonical URLs:** Every page must have one.](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=901.0 "PLAY @15:01")
    - **Heading Hierarchy:** Use a logical structure (e.g., one `<h1>`, then `<h2>`s).
    - [**Image Optimization:**](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=1024.0 "PLAY @17:04")
        - [Use real product/service screenshots, not stock photos.](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=1024.0 "PLAY @17:04")
        - [Use descriptive `alt` text for every image.](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=1140.0 "PLAY @19:00")
        - [Use lowercase, keyword-rich filenames and URLs (e.g., `coworking-space.jpg`).](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=1099.0 "PLAY @18:19")
    - [**Open Graph (OG) Tags:** Implement for social sharing (Twitter, LinkedIn).](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=1082.0 "PLAY @18:02")
    - [**Page Speed:** Ensure fast loading on both mobile and desktop.](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=1209.0 "PLAY @20:09")
    - [**URLs:** Use full, absolute URLs (e.g., `https://www.example.com/page`) to avoid redirects.](https://fathom.video/share/RYC3-usSSfC3nX3Bzq71nTrDSjmDX_JU?tab=summary&timestamp=1234.0 "PLAY @20:34")
